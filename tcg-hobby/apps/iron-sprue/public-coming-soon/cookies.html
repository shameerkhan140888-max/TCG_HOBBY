Cookie policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

This coming-soon page does not set analytics, advertising, account, basket, checkout or tracking cookies.

Essential technologies

The page may use essential hosting and security technologies provided by Cloudflare. These protect the site, keep pages available and help prevent abusive traffic.

Launch-list form

The launch-list form submits directly to an Iron Sprue Cloudflare Pages Function. It does not use browser local storage and does not set a signup cookie.

Future storefront

When the full Iron Sprue storefront opens, account, basket and checkout features may require essential cookies. Any analytics or advertising cookies will be explained and controlled where required before they are used.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies using your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may affect future account, basket or checkout features once the storefront opens.
