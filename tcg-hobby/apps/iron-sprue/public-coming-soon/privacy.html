Iron Sprue is a trading division of Capital Hobby Group Ltd. This temporary static page is used to share launch information before the full storefront opens.

Launch-list signups

If you join the launch list, we collect your email address, your consent record and basic technical request information needed to process the signup safely. We use this only for Iron Sprue launch updates, new-stock announcements and occasional product news.

Launch-list records are stored in the dedicated Iron Sprue Neon database. Confirmation and unsubscribe emails are sent through Resend. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link included in each launch-list email or by contacting us.

Information this page does not collect

This page does not process payments, create customer accounts, record orders, run analytics, publish basket or checkout routes, or expose the full Iron Sprue storefront.
